Two ways to work, for two different questions:

  • Agent Workspace — “do this one thing to my project”. It inspects, proposes, and waits for your approval.
  • Workflow Studio — “build me a process I can keep”. A typed graph you can save as a native .model3, edit and re-run.

Nothing touches your project until you click Apply or Run. Inspections are read-only, proposals are inert, and results arrive as temporary layers.
